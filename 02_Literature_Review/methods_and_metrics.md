# Methods and evaluation measures from the literature

The reviewed studies use systematic reviews, organisational case studies, framework design, behavioural models, maturity assessment, expert judgement, multi-criteria decision methods and technical experiments. Because their evidence sources differ, one reported score cannot be compared directly across all studies.

For this proposal, the minimum measure set is:

- risk-record completeness;
- applicable-control coverage;
- treatment traceability;
- residual-risk reduction;
- repeatability of scoring; and
- improvement over a basic checklist baseline.

These measures are defined before scenario evaluation to avoid deciding success only after results are seen.
