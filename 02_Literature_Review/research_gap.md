# Research gap and proposed response

The earlier 40-study SLR identified five recurring gaps:

1. Limited longitudinal and multi-sector validation.
2. Inconsistent evaluation measures across risk, compliance, maturity and technical studies.
3. Weak links between policy compliance and observable security outcomes.
4. Frameworks that do not adapt quickly enough to emerging technology.
5. Limited evidence showing when framework tailoring improves security rather than only reducing documentation.

This proposal focuses on gaps 2 and 5. Existing studies provide useful standards, models and individual cases, but there is limited repeatable evidence connecting CIA impact, risk priority, framework outcomes, treatment ownership and evaluation across different SME contexts.

The proposed framework responds with:

- separate confidentiality, integrity and availability impact scores;
- one traceable record from asset to residual risk;
- transparent ISO/IEC and NIST outcome mapping;
- explicit treatment owner and evidence fields;
- a common evaluation set for three synthetic scenarios; and
- comparison with a basic unstructured checklist.
